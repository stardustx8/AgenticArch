# Case brief

## Goal and acceptance criteria

State the user-visible outcome. Assign stable requirement IDs and expected evidence.

## Constraints and exclusions

State non-negotiable constraints, allowed side effects and decisions already settled.

## Current evidence

Identify the target baseline and snapshot, relevant files/versions, failed attempts and check results.

## Questions requiring Pro/Fable

State the genuinely hard decisions rather than asking for an unspecified redesign.

## Omitted context and unknown local facts

Record all redactions/omissions and the implications for the reviewers.
